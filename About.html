OUR STORY

How it all began.

Well, it was an instinct I got when I was making chocolates for Diwali Celebration at my home. I had a thought in my mind for years to open a restaurant to set my cravings for a foodie cook! But, I analyzed that most of the delicious dishes I make are from the genre - "CHOCOLATE"!! Then a bell rang in my head, let's just start from here only. I said, Why Not?? And here I am..... From basics to advanced, I am onto the path of new innovative flavors and tastes of chocolates. I am open to new suggestions and focus more on improving day by day. Making chocolates is really my mood-maker, my positive energy and looking at all those happy faces after eating my chocolates is seriously a BLISS!!!

01 — OUR PRODUCTS

Are all your products handmade?

Yes, every item on Crafting Gifts is 100% handmade with love and attention to detail.

Do you offer customization?

Yes, we specialize in personalized gifting. You can share your preferences while ordering or contact us directly.

Can I see a sample before placing a bulk order?

Yes, you can contact us to discuss samples and customization options before placing a bulk order.

What kind of occasions do you cater to?

We create gifts for birthdays, anniversaries, weddings, festivals and other special occasions.

Can I include a personalized message or name?

Yes! You can request a name, message or other personalized details while placing your order.

02 — RETURNS & EXCHANGES

Can I cancel my order after placing it?

Contact us as soon as possible. Cancellation depends on whether your order has already been prepared.

What if I receive a damaged or incorrect product?

Please contact us with photographs of the product so we can help resolve the issue.

Do you accept returns or exchanges?

Since our products are handmade and personalized, return and exchange requests are reviewed individually.

How will I receive my refund?

If a refund is approved, we will inform you about the refund process and expected timeline.

03 — SHIPPING & DELIVERY

Do you deliver all over India?

Please contact us with your delivery PIN code to confirm whether we deliver to your location.

What is the estimated delivery time?

Delivery time depends on your location and the time required to prepare your handmade order.

Will I receive tracking details?

If tracking is available for your shipment, we will share the details with you.

Do you offer express delivery?

Contact us before ordering to check whether express delivery is available for your order.
